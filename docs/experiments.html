E01 in detail: embedding model comparison

What was done

  1. Read the OpenRouter embedding model list and prices (free call). Candidates: the five listed on the owner's model list that are available as embedding endpoints.
  2. Split the 24 knowledge-base articles into 167 chunks. Sections are split at headings, then packed to at most 800 characters with 100 characters of overlap.
  3. Took the dev questions with a gold quote (16 of 20 dev questions; the other 4 are unanswerable). A chunk is relevant when it contains the gold quote, after whitespace and case normalisation.
  4. Embedded every chunk and every question with each model, with no query instruction for any model. Scored by cosine similarity, top 5.
  5. Metrics: recall@5 (is a relevant chunk in the top 5) and nDCG@5 (binary relevance). Bootstrap 95% CI, 2000 resamples, seed 0.
  6. Recorded actual token usage from OpenRouter's response for each model, and the cost from its listed price.

Caveats

  • 16 answerable questions is a small sample. The intervals overlap, so no model is clearly better than another.
  • The choice is provisional. The test split confirms it once, in the final step.
  • Only dense retrieval was compared. BM25 and fusion are a separate experiment (M2).
