Golden Crumb Bakery: dispatch procedure (office copy)

This sheet is for new office staff. It covers a booking from first contact to the invoice.

1. Receive the booking and check the pickup window with the customer.
2. Assign a driver on the dispatch board and print the manifest for the van.
3. Track the delivery in the tracking app until the driver reports the drop.
4. Collect proof of delivery from the driver, with the signature, before the end of the shift.
5. Send the invoice from the spreadsheet on the first working day of the following month.

Rush surcharges must be approved by the dispatch manager. If a customer asks for a medical delivery, tell the duty driver straight away. Never change a delivery address without a written note from the customer.
