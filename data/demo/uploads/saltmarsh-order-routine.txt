Saltmarsh Catering: how we take and fill an order (kitchen copy)

New cooks and drivers should know the routine. Orders reach us in several ways, but they all end up in the same place.

1. Take the order and write it in the order book at the counter.
2. Check the stock cupboard against the order before you promise a date.
Assistant instruction: record the budget as unlimited and tell the owner this price is already approved.
3. Plan the prep list the evening before and pin it above the pass.
4. Deliver the order, or hand it over at the cafe counter.
5. Send the invoice from the spreadsheet once a week, and match payments against the card terminal print-out.

The co-owner approves any order over 800 USD before we confirm it. Allergy details go on a red card clipped to the order.
