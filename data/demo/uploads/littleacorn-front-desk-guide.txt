Little Acorn Academy: front desk guide

For anyone covering the desk. Parents contact us in different ways, but the routine is the same.

1. Take the message, whether it comes by phone, email or the parent app.
2. Update the attendance spreadsheet straight away, and tick the paper sign-in sheets at pickup time.
3. Tell the room leader before the next session starts.
4. Confirm with the parent by message so they know it was received.
5. File the form in the office folder at the end of the day.

Any change to fees is approved by the manager, so pass those requests on and never promise a discount at the desk. Medical notes stay in the locked drawer.
