# Urgent requests and out-of-hours work

Some businesses receive requests that cannot wait until morning. This article explains why we ask about them, what counts as urgent, and how the modules treat an urgent request differently from a normal one.

## Why we ask

An automated system that treats a gas smell like a routine quote is dangerous. Before any module is recommended, the interview asks whether urgent requests exist, what counts as urgent for you, and who takes those calls today. The answers decide whether a module may handle a request on its own or must pass it to a person at once.

## What counts as urgent

You decide. For a heating firm it might be no heat in winter or a smell of gas. For a clinic it might be a patient in pain. For a shop it might be nothing at all; many businesses have no urgent requests and say so. The assistant records your definition in your own words.

## How the modules treat urgent requests

The phone-intake module passes an urgent call to a person immediately, using the handover you agree during setup, and does not try to book it. The email-triage module gives an urgent message the highest priority label so it sits at the top of the inbox. The ticket-creation module creates the ticket with the urgent flag so that staff-assignment can suggest the on-call person. None of the modules decides on its own that something is not urgent; if the request matches your definition, it is treated as urgent.

## Out-of-hours work

If you have an on-call rota, we ask who is on it and how they are reached today. The modules use that rota; they do not create one. If nobody takes night calls today, the report says so and treats it as a gap for you to decide on, not as something a module solves by itself.

## What this means for the estimate

Urgent handling does not change the rate card price. It changes the setup: the handover rules and the urgent labels are agreed during the setup period covered by the setup fee. The report lists them under the recommended modules so that nothing about urgent handling is left implicit.

## If you are not sure

Say so. The assistant records the field as unknown, moves on, and the report lists it under unknowns and next steps, with the question that would close it.
