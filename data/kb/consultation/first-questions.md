# The first questions we ask

The discovery interview follows a fixed list of questions. This article explains why the list exists, what the first questions are, and what the assistant does with your answers.

## Why a fixed list

A fixed list means every owner is asked the same things in the same order, and the report can say which questions were answered and which were not. The assistant may phrase an acknowledgement in its own words, but the question it asks next comes from the list, word for word. It never decides on its own that a topic is finished.

## The company questions

The interview starts with the company: what it is called, what kind of business it is, how many people work there, how many locations you run, and who we are talking to. These are short answers and they set the context for everything after.

## The workflow questions

Next comes the workflow: how requests reach you, roughly how many come in on a normal day, how many minutes one request takes to handle, how many minutes of typing or re-keying follow, which tools you use, and the steps from arrival to done. We also ask whether anything in the process needs an approval.

## The problem questions

Then the problems: what goes wrong most often, what kind of mistakes happen, which parts you would like to hand over, and what matters most to you, whether it is saving time, fewer errors, lower cost or room to grow.

## The constraint questions

Finally the constraints: whether your tools can export, import or offer an API, whether you handle personal data, whether urgent requests exist, your budget range if you have one, and when you would want to start.

## What happens to an answer

Each answer becomes a fact with a status. A fact you state is marked reported. A fact you confirm when the assistant asks you to check it is marked confirmed. A fact the assistant derives, for example a daily volume from a monthly one, is marked estimated. If you say you do not know, the fact is marked unknown and the question is not asked again. Every fact shows the sentence it came from.

## If you ask for the price early

The assistant will not give a number until the assessment questions are answered. It will tell you what it still needs and continue. This is not evasion; it is how the estimate stays honest.
