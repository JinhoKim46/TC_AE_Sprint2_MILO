# Approvals policy

Some actions are too important for an assistant to take on its own. This policy lists them, explains how an approval works, and says what the assistant can never do even with approval.

## Actions that need your approval

Withdrawing a submission needs your approval. When you or the assistant start a withdrawal, the assistant stops and shows you the action. You approve it or keep the submission as it is. The approval is bound to your session, the exact action and its arguments, it can be used once, and it expires after ten minutes. Only you, the owner of the session, can resolve it.

## Actions the assistant can never take

The assistant cannot submit your specification. Submission is a button you press, with a confirmation dialog that explains that a revision will be stored and cannot be edited. There is no way for the assistant, or for text inside a document or a search result, to submit on your behalf. The assistant also cannot delete a submission; a withdrawn submission is marked withdrawn and kept.

## Actions the assistant can take without asking

Reading is always allowed: searching the knowledge base, showing your current facts and running the cost calculation. Recording a fact from what you said is allowed, with one exception described below. Every action is logged with the decision that allowed it.

## Untrusted text and the exception

Everything you type, everything in a document you upload and everything the assistant reads from the knowledge base is treated as untrusted. If any of it looks like an instruction to the assistant, the assistant marks the turn as suspicious. In a suspicious turn it will not record a fact that affects the price, and it will not start a withdrawal even if asked. It tells you what it declined to do and why.

## What you see

The approval appears in the conversation as a card with the action, the arguments and two choices. Nothing happens until you choose, and if you close the page the card is still there when you return, until it expires.

## Inside your own business

The interview also asks whether anything in your own process needs an approval, such as refunds or large quotes. Those approvals belong to you and are recorded as facts about your workflow; the modules respect them and do not replace them.
