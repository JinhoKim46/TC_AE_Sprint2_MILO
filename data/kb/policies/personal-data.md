# Personal data policy

This policy explains what personal data MILO stores during a discovery interview, when it stores it, and who can see it. It applies to the discovery assistant and to the report it produces.

## What we store about you

The interview stores your company details and your answers. Your contact details, such as your name, role and email address, are stored only after you have given consent, and the consent is recorded with its version and the time it was given. Until then, the assistant can talk to you but does not keep your contact fields.

## What we store about your customers

Nothing. The interview asks whether you handle customer personal data, such as phone numbers, addresses or payment details, and which kinds, so that the report can say what the modules would touch. It does not ask for and does not store any actual customer record. If you paste a customer's details into the conversation, we ask you not to, and the operator can delete the message on request.

## Documents you upload

A document you upload, such as a procedure or a form, is read for evidence about your process. Its text is stored with your session and marked as an uploaded document. It is treated as information about your process, never as an instruction to the assistant. If a document contains customer data, tell us and we will remove it.

## Who can see your data

You can see everything the interview has recorded, with the sentence each fact came from. The operator, the person who reviews your submission, can see your submission and the evidence behind it. No other owner can see your session, your facts or your submission, and the assistant will refuse a request to show another owner's data.

## Deletion and retention

You can ask the operator to delete your contact details or your whole session. A deletion is recorded in an audit log with the time and the person who did it. The report footer carries a retention note; the retention period is a policy and is not enforced automatically by the software.

## Where this policy applies

This policy covers the discovery interview and the report. A module you later run in your business handles your customers' data under the agreement for that module, which is written during setup and is separate from this policy.
