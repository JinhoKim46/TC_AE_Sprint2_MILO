# Document data entry

The document-data-entry module reads paper forms and scanned sheets and enters the fields into the system of record, flagging anything unclear. It is for businesses where jobs are written on paper first and typed into a tool later.

## What it does

You scan or photograph a form. The module finds the fields on it, such as customer name, address, job type and date, and enters them into your system of record. Each field passes through validation rules that you agree with us, for example that a date must be a real date. Anything the module cannot read with confidence is flagged for a person to check, rather than guessed.

## What is included

The module includes field extraction, validation rules, flagged items for review, and an entry log. The entry log shows which form produced which record, so a wrong entry can be traced back to the sheet it came from.

## What is not included

The document-data-entry module does not read handwriting outside the agreed form types. It does not do signature verification. If your team uses a form we have not set up, those sheets are flagged rather than entered.

## Connecting it to your tools

The module works with csv-export and with export-only-tool. When your tool can only export and not import, entries are pushed through a file drop or prepared for re-keying, as described in the implementation article on export-only tools.

## Price

The base monthly price for document-data-entry is 490 USD. The setup fee is 900 USD per module. If your tool is export-only, a monthly surcharge of 250 USD applies for the extra handling. All prices are indicative and non-binding.

## Who it suits

Owners whose office re-keys paper job sheets at the end of the day, and who see wrong addresses or missed jobs as a result, usually start here. The flagged-items queue keeps a person in charge of anything doubtful.
