# Staff assignment

The staff-assignment module suggests which staff member should take a ticket, using the skills and availability the owner has entered. It is a suggestion, not an order: a person can always override it.

## What it does

You enter a short list of skill tags for each member of staff, such as boiler, drains, grooming or front desk, and their working hours. When a ticket is created, the module compares the ticket's request type with the skill tags and the availability, and suggests one person. The suggestion comes with reasons, for example that the person has the matching skill and is free that afternoon.

## What is included

The module includes skill tags, availability, a suggestion with reasons, and override by staff. The override is logged so that you can see how often the suggestion was changed.

## What is not included

The staff-assignment module does not make automatic payroll or shift changes. It does not make decisions about discipline. It only proposes who is best placed to take a job; everything about pay, shifts and conduct stays with you.

## Connecting it to your tools

The module works with csv-export. Skill tags and availability can be imported from a spreadsheet, and the assignments can be exported to one.

## Price

The base monthly price for staff-assignment is 340 USD. The setup fee is 900 USD per module. The csv-export integration has no monthly surcharge. All prices are indicative and non-binding.

## Who it suits

Owners who dispatch several vans or several chairs by hand, and who find themselves asking who is free, usually add this module after ticket-creation. It works best when the skill tags are kept simple and up to date.
