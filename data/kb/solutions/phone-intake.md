# AI phone intake

The phone-intake module answers incoming calls out of hours and in peak times, records the request, and books or passes it on. It is built for businesses where the phone rings while every member of staff is busy with a customer.

## What it does

When a call comes in, the module answers, asks what the caller needs and captures the request as text. If the caller wants an appointment, it books one in the slots you have opened. If the request needs a person, it writes a handover note and passes the call to a member of staff. Every call produces a transcript that stays with the request.

## What is included

The module includes call answering, request capture, appointment booking, a call transcript for each call, and handover to staff. The transcript is the evidence for every fact the module records, so a member of staff can check what the caller actually said.

## What is not included

The phone-intake module does not make outbound sales calls. It does not give medical or legal advice, and it does not take card payments by phone. If a caller asks for any of these, the module explains that a person will follow up.

## Connecting it to your tools

The module works with the csv-export integration and the api-connector integration. With csv-export, booked requests are written to a file that your booking tool can import. With api-connector, the module writes each booking straight into your tool through its API.

## Price

The base monthly price for phone-intake is 690 USD. The setup fee is 900 USD per module. The monthly price is multiplied by a volume factor that depends on how many requests you handle a day; see the pricing section for the tiers. All prices are indicative and non-binding.

## Who it suits

Owners whose main problem is missed calls at peak times, or who want bookings taken when the shop is closed, usually start here. The module records what the caller asked for, so nothing is lost between the call and the booking.
