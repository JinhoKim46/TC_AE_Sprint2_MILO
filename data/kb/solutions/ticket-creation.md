# Ticket creation

The ticket-creation module turns each accepted request into a ticket with the fields the team needs, and tracks its status. It sits after intake: once a request has been accepted, the module makes sure it becomes a job that someone owns.

## What it does

For each accepted request, the module creates a ticket and fills in the fields your team has agreed on, such as customer, address, request type and requested date. Before it creates a ticket it checks for duplicates, so the same request sent twice does not become two jobs. It then tracks the ticket's status from open to done and reminds staff when a ticket has not moved.

## What is included

The module includes ticket fields, a duplicate check, status tracking and reminders to staff. The reminders go to the staff member who owns the ticket, not to the customer.

## What is not included

The ticket-creation module does not close a ticket without a person. A member of staff marks the job done. It does not do billing; invoices stay in your existing system.

## Connecting it to your tools

The module works with csv-export, api-connector and crm-connector. The crm-connector maps ticket fields to the fields in your CRM, so a ticket created here appears as a record there.

## Price

The base monthly price for ticket-creation is 290 USD. The setup fee is 900 USD per module. If you use the crm-connector, a monthly surcharge of 450 USD applies for the connector. All prices are indicative and non-binding.

## Who it suits

Owners who write jobs on paper or in a spreadsheet and then lose track of which ones are done usually pair this module with phone-intake or email-triage. The ticket is the single place where the job lives.
