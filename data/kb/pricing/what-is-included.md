# What is included and what is not

A monthly price covers a fixed list of things. This article says what that list is, and what sits outside it, so that there are no surprises after the first invoice.

## Included in the monthly price

The monthly price for a module covers the running of that module for your business: the software, the hosting, the templates and rules we agree with you during setup, and the audit or delivery logs that each module keeps. It also covers the integration you chose, once the integration surcharge is added. Minor changes to wording, labels or skill tags are included; you can ask for them at any time.

## Included in the setup fee

The setup fee pays for the work of getting a module live: agreeing the form types, templates or skill tags, connecting the module to your tool, and a short period of checking the first results with you. The setup fee is paid once per module.

## Not included

Prices exclude taxes, travel and any hardware. If a module needs a phone number, a scanner or a device at your premises, that is bought separately. Changes that go beyond the module's scope, for example a new form type after setup, are quoted as additional work.

## Scope limits per module

Each module has a short list of things it does not do, written in its own article. For example, email-triage never sends a message without approval, and ticket-creation never closes a ticket without a person. These limits are part of the price: a module that did more would cost more and would carry more risk.

## No promised savings

The estimate is indicative and non-binding. We do not promise a saving in hours or money. The report that follows the discovery interview gives a simple return estimate built from the minutes you told us, with each input labelled, and you decide whether it is worth it.

## Checking the list

If you are unsure whether something is included, ask the discovery assistant. It will show you the article it relies on, and if the article does not answer, it will say so instead of guessing.
