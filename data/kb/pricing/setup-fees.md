# Setup fees and integration surcharges

Two parts of the price are easy to overlook: the one-time setup fee and the monthly surcharge for the integration your tools need. This article explains both.

## The setup fee

The setup fee is 900 USD per module and is paid once. If you choose two modules, two setup fees apply. The fee covers the work of agreeing templates, rules or skill tags with you, connecting the module to your tool, and checking the first results together.

## Integration surcharges

The monthly surcharge depends on how the module talks to your existing tool.

| Integration | Monthly surcharge |
|---|---|
| csv-export | 0 USD |
| api-connector | 300 USD |
| crm-connector | 450 USD |
| export-only-tool | 250 USD |

The csv-export integration has no monthly surcharge, because your tool does the import and export itself. The api-connector surcharge is 300 USD a month and covers keeping the live connection working as your tool changes. The crm-connector surcharge is 450 USD a month and includes the field mapping. The export-only-tool surcharge is 250 USD a month and covers the file drop or prepared re-keying that an export-only tool needs.

## How surcharges combine

A surcharge is counted once per integration, not once per module. If three modules all use the same api-connector, you pay the surcharge once. If you use two different integrations, both surcharges apply.

## Which integration do you have

The discovery assistant asks whether your tools can export, import, or offer an API, and picks the integration from your answer. If you are not sure, say so; the estimate will then be marked as a scenario with the assumption shown, rather than as an indicative figure.

## Nothing hidden

There are no other fees on the rate card. Taxes, travel and hardware are excluded, as the article on what is included explains. All figures are indicative and non-binding.
